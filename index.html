One thread runs through my career: designing resilient architectures
that scale without interruption and support millions of real-time events.

At IPCOM I took on a CPaaS platform that needed to process over a
million messages a day. I built the messaging core in Go + NATS,
implemented dynamic RBAC, migrated SIP components to PJSIP, and
cut MTTR by 35% with a new observability layer.

At BYONDIT I modernized Tigo Money's banking core. I designed the
REST layer connecting SOAP systems to mobile channels across three
LATAM markets — zero-downtime migrations, device-level zero-trust auth.

As an independent consultant, I've worked with teams whose CI/CD
pipelines barely managed one build a day. Dependency analysis and
caching turned that into 20+ builds a day, same quota, same cost.

I'm finishing my Systems Engineering degree while working full-time —
a sign of how I operate under competing constraints and still deliver.
